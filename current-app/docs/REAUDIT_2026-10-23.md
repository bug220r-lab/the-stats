## Dashboard use

Start with **Overview** to compare activity, inspect the current leading recording and follow a real shortcut to songs, social content or the decision report. **Comparison** offers loaded-date presets, prior-year YTD, all history and custom dates. YTD shortcuts carry current dates; the destination song/decision pages explicitly compare the preceding equal-length interval.

**Spotify** retains artist history, distinct recordings, release ages, audience/source hierarchies and recovery evidence. **Social** uses the approved follows series and supports account/scope/outcome filters, content search and source-post links. **YouTube collection** offers searchable inventory, video drilldown and native activity-date filters. The separate supplement provides full daily watch time, AVD and subscriber series. Never add its overlapping values to the parent.

**Decision report** recalculates descriptive results for selected dates. Its full report download includes all recordings; the song search has its own filtered CSV. Lifetime posts, release-age tables, geography and historical inference explicitly retain their own source windows. **Campaigns** retains its template, validation, immutable revision history and editable-current export; corrected real metadata needs a later revision timestamp.

**Saved sources & versions → Reload saved data** clears cached reports and rereads local files. It does not collect fresh platform data. **Import data** previews and commits a new local snapshot. A dated report recalculation also does not recollect data or rerun historical inference. Source details, hashes and lineage remain available in secondary expanders and data-quality views.
